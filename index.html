Conversion copy — landing page + email sequence

Spec work for "Numa," a fictional sleep app. Written as a complete funnel: one landing page, a 3-email welcome sequence, and a social caption — each piece with one job.

Landing page (excerpt)

H1: Fall asleep like you mean it.

Sub: Numa doesn't just track your sleep. It trains the hour before it — so you stop lying awake wondering why you can't switch off.

Problem section: "You're not bad at sleeping. You just never told your brain the day ended."

Most nights it's not the coffee or the stress that keeps you up. It's that your body walked from 9pm doomscrolling straight into bed, and your nervous system never got the signal to downshift. Sleep apps watch what happens after midnight. Numa fixes the hour before it.

Welcome email 1 of 3 — Day 0

Subject: Your evening just got an off-switch

You didn't download Numa because you want more sleep data. You want more sleep. Here's the one thing to do tonight: open Numa at 9:30pm and do the 2-minute brain dump. Don't set a goal. Don't fix your schedule. Just dump what's in your head and hit "done for today."

That single action tells your brain the day ended. Everything else we do together builds on that.

Social caption

Your alarm goes off at 6am and you're still here at 11:47pm. Not because you're not tired. Because your brain never got the memo that the day ended.

Editing — AI draft to publishable copy

The core of my daily workflow: a raw AI draft, then a line-by-line edit.

Before — raw AI draft

"Leveraging a global Employer of Record (EOR) empowers organizations to streamline their international hiring processes while ensuring full compliance with local labor regulations — enabling businesses to scale their workforce across borders without the administrative burden of establishing local entities."

After — edited

"Hiring someone in another country usually means opening a local company there. That takes months and costs money. An Employer of Record (EOR) does it for you instead. The EOR hires the person on your behalf, handles payroll and taxes, and keeps you compliant with local labor laws. You get the worker on your team. They handle the paperwork."

Cut the expensive words, cut the em dashes, one idea per sentence, concrete over abstract.

Short-form scripts

Waterllama (water tracker)

Hook: "You're not dehydrated. You're just bored of water."

You don't log water here — you fill up a llama. Streaks, characters to collect, a little celebration the moment you hit your goal. It's a game that happens to hydrate you.

Foodllama (food tracker)

Hook: "Food tracking doesn't have to feel like getting graded."

Every calorie app feels like a report card. You log one snack and it basically says "shame on you." So you quit by Thursday.
